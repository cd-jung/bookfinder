# Results and Book Details

## Reading the results

Above the results you see how many books were found. Each book card shows the cover, title, author,
year, language, rating, lowest price and whether it is **In stock** or **Out of stock**. Books without a
cover image show a placeholder.

## Sorting and pages

Use **Sort by** to order the results by **Relevance**, **Newest**, **Oldest**, **Title A-Z**,
**Highest rated** or **Price low-high**. Results are shown 20 per page; use the page numbers below the
results to move between pages.

## Grid and list view

Switch between the grid and list view with the two view buttons next to **Sort by**. BookFinder remembers
your choice on this device.

## Opening a book

Click a book card (or press **Enter** on it) to open its details:

- cover, title, authors, description, ISBN, pages, publisher, publication date and language
- every format with its price, and **In stock** or **Out of stock** (with the expected date when we know it)
- **Similar books**: click one to open it

Click an author's name to search for all their books. Use **Copy ISBN** or **Copy link** to copy the ISBN
or a link that opens this book directly. Close the details with the **x**, **Esc** or by clicking outside.

If a link points to a book that no longer exists, you see **Book not found** and can keep searching.
