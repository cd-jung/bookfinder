# Searching for Books

Open **Book Search** and stay on the **Keyword & filters** tab.

## Searching by keyword

1. Type words in the **Search books** box. The search looks at the title, author, ISBN, publisher and description.
2. Press **Enter** or click the search icon.
3. To clear the keyword, click the **x** in the box.

To find one book quickly, type its ISBN. Hyphens are optional; the matching book is shown first.

## Using filters

Filters are on the left of the results (on a phone, tap **Filters**, choose your filters and tap **Apply**).

- **Genre**: tick one or more genres. Books in any ticked genre are shown. The number next to each genre is how many current results it has.
- **Author**: type at least 2 letters and pick an author from the list.
- **Publication year**: enter a **From** and **To** year. If **From** is after **To**, you see an error and the filter is not applied.
- **Language** and **Format** (Hardcover, Paperback, eBook, Audiobook).
- **In stock only**: hide books that cannot be bought right now.
- **Minimum rating**: show books rated at least that many stars.
- **Price**: drag the two handles of the price slider.

Different filters are combined: a book must match all of them.

## Removing filters

Each active filter is shown as a chip above the results. Click the **x** on a chip to remove that filter,
or click **Clear all** to remove every filter and the keyword.

## When nothing matches

If no book matches, you see **No books match**. Click **Remove last filter** to undo your last filter, or
**Get help** to ask the Help assistant for search tips.
