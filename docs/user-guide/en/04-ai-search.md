# Searching in Plain Language (AI)

Open **Book Search** and choose the **Ask in plain language (AI)** tab.

## Asking a question

1. Type what you are looking for, for example `Korean novels after 2015 under $20 with 4+ stars`, or click one of the example questions.
2. Press **Enter** or click the sparkle button. While the question is translated, the box is disabled.
3. Read **Interpreted as**: it lists every condition BookFinder understood. Check that it matches what you meant.

Questions can be up to 500 characters long. You can also combine your question with the filters on the left
to narrow the results further.

## What you can ask about

You can ask about the title, author, ISBN, publisher, genre, language, format, publication year or date,
pages, price, rating, number of ratings and stock, and about award winners, series books, new releases
and books similar to another book.

## Editing the conditions

Click **Edit as filters** to copy the understood conditions into the filters and switch to the
**Keyword & filters** tab. Conditions the filters cannot show are listed in a notice.

## Messages you may see

- **The question could not be translated. Please rephrase it.**: the question is not about books, or asks for something BookFinder cannot search.
- **The AI search is unavailable. Use keyword search instead.**: try again later or use the keyword search.
- **Search took too long, try a narrower question**: add more conditions to your question.
- **Too many questions, please wait N seconds**: you asked more than 10 questions in a minute.

When nothing matches, click **Try keyword search** to switch back to the keyword search. Switching to the
**Keyword & filters** tab also removes the AI conditions.
