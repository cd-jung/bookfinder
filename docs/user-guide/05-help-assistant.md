# Help Assistant

The Help assistant explains how to use BookFinder. It answers only from this User Guide; it does not
answer questions about individual books (use the search for that) or general questions.

## Asking a question

1. Click **Help** in the bottom-right corner.
2. Type your question and press **Enter**, or click one of the suggested questions. Press **Shift+Enter** for a new line.
3. Read the short answer, then click a link below it to open the full User Guide section.

Questions can be up to 1000 characters long; a counter appears near the limit. You can ask a follow-up
question: the assistant takes your previous question into account.

While the assistant is looking up the answer, you can click **Stop generating** to cancel.

## When the assistant cannot answer

If the User Guide does not cover your question, the assistant says
"I couldn't find that in the User Guide" instead of guessing. Try other words, or browse the User Guide menu.

## Copying and rating answers

Click the copy button to copy an answer. Click the thumbs up or thumbs down button to tell us whether an
answer helped; after a thumbs down you can add a comment and click **Submit**.

## Your conversation

The conversation stays when you close and reopen the panel, and when you reload the page in the same tab.
Click **New conversation** and confirm with **Clear** to start again.

## When something goes wrong

If the assistant is unavailable or takes longer than 30 seconds, an error message is shown with a
**Retry** button. If you ask too many questions in a minute, you are asked to wait a few seconds.
