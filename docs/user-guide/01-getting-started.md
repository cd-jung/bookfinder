# Getting Started

BookFinder helps you find books in our catalogue of 10,000 titles. You do not need an account.

## Finding your way around

The left menu has four sections:

- **Book Search**: search the catalogue by keyword, with filters, or in plain language.
- **User Guide**: these pages.
- **Logs & Monitoring**: request logs and service health.
- **Coverage**: how much of BookFinder is covered by automated tests, shown inside the app.

Click **BookFinder** at the top to start a new, empty search. On a small screen, open the menu with the
menu button in the top-left corner.

## Sharing and bookmarking a search

The address in your browser keeps your search: the keyword, filters, sort order, page and the open
book. Copy the address to share the same results, or bookmark it. **Back** and **Forward** in your
browser return to your previous searches, and reloading the page keeps your search.

## Getting help

Click **Help** in the bottom-right corner to ask how to do something in BookFinder. The assistant answers
from this User Guide and links to the section it used.

## Using the keyboard

Every action works with the keyboard. Press **Tab** to move between controls and **Enter** or **Space** to
open a book or press a button. **Esc** closes the book details and the Help panel.

## When something goes wrong

- **Could not load books**: the search could not reach the server. Press **Retry**.
- **You are offline**: search is turned off until your connection is back.
- **Still loading...**: the search is taking longer than usual. Wait a moment.
